New unplanned outages first seen each day. Each line starts the day after we began capturing that DNO.

How long unplanned power cuts last

From when the fault was reported to when it left the feed. Outages whose end time is uncertain by more than 30 minutes are left out.

How accurate are restoration estimates?

When power came back compared with the last estimate given. On time means within 30 minutes either side. First estimate on time scores the original estimate instead.
